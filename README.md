## Project Overview

Lung adenocarcinoma (LUAD) is a malignant cancer arising from glandular epithelial cells and is one of the most lethal cancer types. LUAD patients can be classified into three molecular subtypes:

- **Terminal Respiratory Unit (TRU)**  
- **Proximal Inflammatory (PI)**  
- **Proximal Proliferative (PP)**  

These subtypes are associated with differential expression of genes relevant to LUAD biology (Ho et al., 2021).  

The goal of this project is twofold:

1. Identify genes with **significant differential expression** across the three subtypes.  
2. Use **machine learning** techniques to predict clinical features of patients based on gene expression patterns.  

The ultimate aim is to identify potential **genetic markers** that could assist in diagnosis, prognosis, and development of targeted therapies for LUAD.
